# Design note: heat pump Aurora and wind turbine Boreal

> Illustrative document. The machines, the company and the figures are imaginary. The claims were written to exercise `limina check` and come from no real product.

## Auxiliary heat engine

The auxiliary heat engine runs between a hot reservoir at 600 K and a cold reservoir at 300 K, with a thermal efficiency of 60%.

## Heat pump

The Aurora heat pump reaches a COP of 14.5 between 300 K and 280 K.

## Wind turbine

With an unshrouded rotor, the Boreal turbine reaches a power coefficient of 0.45.

The Boreal turbine, with a shrouded rotor, reaches a power coefficient of 0.70 in gusty conditions.
