# Design: two-model-laws
Status: v1, frozen.

- `limina-dsl`: `compare.rs` (structure check of direction, bounded kind and parameters; 200 SplitMix64 samples, log-uniform when the range spans more than two decades; the decisive bound is the largest bound over the configurations for Max and the smallest for Min, so two writers that split the same law into assumptions differently still agree; equal typed errors at a sample count as agreement); `curve.rs` (`bound_curve`, 2 to 1000 points, undefined points are null).
- `limina-extract`: `law.rs` (`identify_law`, `author_two` running both models concurrently, `extract_parameters` with the evidence rules of `check.rs`, `transcribe_image`); `VisionProvider` and `ImageInput` in `provider.rs`, implemented by `OpenAiProvider` (60 s timeout); `LIMINA_LLM_MODEL_B` and `LIMINA_LLM_VISION_MODEL` in `config.rs`. One rule added to the extraction prompt: claims no limit covers are listed with `limit_id` null.
- `limina-api`: `pipeline.rs` runs the four steps after the existing extraction and evaluation, merges a null-limit reading of the same number at the same place as a built-in claim (trace step `deduplication`), registers only agreed laws, and serves `/v1/curve`, `/v1/transcribe`, `/v1/pipeline`. New error `InvalidCurveRequest` (422).
- `web/`: `workbench.ts` sends each paragraph to `/v1/analyze` (an unusable answer is asked again, 3 tries), shifts offsets to the whole text, calls `/v1/solve` for IMPOSSIBLE and CONDITIONAL and `/v1/evaluate` per mode for COP without a mode; `explorer.ts` draws `/v1/curve`; `workbench-logic.ts` holds the pure helpers.
- Models (measured on the NVIDIA endpoint): A `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning-30b-a3b`, B `nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b`, vision `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl-120b-a12b`. Mistral, GLM and Kimi timed out at 15 s and Gemma answered 404, so both authoring models are Nemotron (deviation: same family).
